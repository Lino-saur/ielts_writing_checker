## 第 23 页

02 流程图题写作方式
范文研读
Provided is the flow diagram illustrating the process of how to change rainwater into drinking water by using different equipment in an Australian town.
There are four main stages in this procedure: collecting, filtration, storage and treatment. The process begins/starts with gathering rainwater as it drips from the roof of the house, and then directed to the next stage through drains. In order to ensure that the rainwater contains less contamination, it must be fully filtered in the second stage. When it comes to the third stage, after being filtered through screens in the filter, the rainwater is delivered to a large container where it is carefully stored.
The rain water is not potable after the first two stages; therefore, it is necessary to conduct the part of treatment before it can be drunk by people. Water treatment is the fourth stage at which some chemicals are added for the sake of hygiene quality and safety. Eventually, the drinking water will be led to residents’ houses through water pipe.
Overall, it can be seen that rainwater can be changed into drinking water through a relatively complicated process.

## 第 30 页

02 流程图题写作方式
范文研读
The diagrams provide information about the different life stages of salmon, including the time each stage takes and the places where salmon live and grow.
In the first stage, salmon lay eggs in the slow-moving upper reaches of the river, where there are reeds and small stones. Hatching takes about 5 to 6 months until small fish, also known as fry, emerge from the eggs. Each baby fish is about 3 to 8 cm in length when it is born. In the next step, these young salmon swim downstream to the lower river, where water flows much faster, and stay there for a period of 4 years. They are termed 'smolt’, each about 12-15 cm long, at this stage.
At the maturation stage, salmon travel to the open sea and spend about 5 years there, during which time they grow into maturity. The size of each fish is about 70-76 cm, around six times as much as that of a smolt. And then, adult fish swim upstream from the sea to the upper river, where their life begins, to deposit eggs. The cycle thus begins anew.
Overall, the whole life cycle of salmon consists of four stages and sees significant changes in the aquatic locations where they live, from fresh water in the first stages to saltwater in the third.

### 演讲者备注

Anew adv.再
Aquatic adj.水生的，水栖的

This track also goes down to the pier where people can go sailing in the south sea of the island. Furthermore, tourists can swim near the beach in the west. A footpath connecting the western accommodation units also leads to the beach.
Overall, comparing the two maps, there are significant changes after this development. Not only lots of facilities are built on the island, but also the sea is used for activities. The new island has become a good place for tourism.
