02 观点类文章解题思路
E.g.
The best way for governments to solve the traffic congestion is to provide free public transport 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
绝对词汇类 (all, none, any, best, most, always, never) – 否定绝对词
一定按照“让步+替代方案”写

### 演讲者备注

【绝对词汇类】
先读题--- 是否翻译best- 是否注意题目中 “最好方法”。在现实生活中说…是最好的是不客观的。所以这类题单独拿出来给大家总结了一类。但是想要学好——
首先要辨别，识别题型，找绝对化表述的词汇，分两个类别（数量上的绝对all, none，any, 程度best, most范围上的绝对always , never也可表程度 ）

The best way for government to solve the traffic congestion is to provide free public transport 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Undoubtedly, the introduction of free public transport will be helpful to ease the traffic.
