## 第 7 页

Line graph 线图
单线图主体写作思路：从古到今
找点：起点，终点，特征点（拐点，最值、阶段值点、交叉点）
交叉点：
相同相遇—A met B at 交点值 in 对应时间.
超过– A surpassed/overtook/exceeded B at 交点值 in 对应时间.
2. *时间顺序连点成线：趋势
1）变化总趋势 （起点和终点）
2）变化的阶段性趋势 (在已找出的特征点处将整条线截3~4个小阶段）
[图片说明：Writing Samples]
The line graph shows estimated  sales of gold in Dubai for 12 months in 2002 in millions of dirhams.

### 演讲者备注

We can see from the line graph that in January, the sales of Dubai gold is 200 millions. After that, the number shoots up until March, peaking at 350. After that, there is a steady decline in the number from March to July. The next month see a dramatic rise in the number again. After August, sales suffers from a drastic fall at 110, which is one of its lowest points. From September to December, the number grows moderately, with the number reaching almost 200 again in December, which is almost the same with number of January.

The sales of gold in Dubai showed a slightly downward trend over the whole year of 2002. More specifically, it climbed drastically from 200 million dirhams to 350 million dirhams in the first two months, reaching the highest point in March. From this time onwards, there was a gradual drop to 110 million dirhams or so from March to July, which hit the bottom.
